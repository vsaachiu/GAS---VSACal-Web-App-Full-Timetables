Hello!

This mini app helps to add / sync classes and duties to your Google Calendar.

Start by entering a date window to work with and choose one of your Google Calendars, or create a new one. Then you can load classes and duties you want to add to that calendar. You can preview events before creating them in Google Calendar. If you have existing events already created by this system on your Google Calendar, you can edit, delete or re-sync them!
